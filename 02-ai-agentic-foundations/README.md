# 02 · Foundations of AI & Agentic AI

A concept module with no code. It follows the progression from traditional AI and machine learning to deep learning, transformers and large language models, and then to agentic AI: systems that plan, use tools and act toward a goal.

## Key concepts

- AI vs. ML vs. deep learning vs. generative AI
- The transformer architecture and self-attention
- How LLMs are trained and where they fail (hallucination, context limits)
- What makes a system *agentic*: perception → reasoning/planning → action → memory
- The agentic AI tech stack: models, orchestration frameworks, tools, memory, guardrails
